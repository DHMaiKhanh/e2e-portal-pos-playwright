## Notes

- **#31 (TC-MCH-003)** is cosmetic — the on-page header/nav already read "Merchants"; only the
  `<title>` tag lags. If you'd rather not treat it as a bug, the alternative is to update the test's
  expected title string; say the word and I'll switch it from `fixme` to a passing assertion.
- **Focus-restore gaps (#19, #25)** are the same WAI-ARIA dialog-pattern issue in two dialogs.
- **#5/#6/#7/#16 (Summary row highlight)** look like one regression in the Summary table's
  selected-row state; fixing the `data-state="selected"` / ARIA wiring likely clears all four.
- **whmcsId handling (#21–#23)** are three facets of the same missing URL-param validation on the
  onboarding Integration-Status route.
